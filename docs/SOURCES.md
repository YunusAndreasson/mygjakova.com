# Gjakova — source library

A curated, annotated bibliography for researching content on this site. Grouped by
authority and language (`[EN] [SQ] [TR]`). Treat **official** and **academic/primary**
entries as citable; treat **press/curated** as leads to verify, and **tertiary**
(encyclopedias) as starting points. Always cross-check dates against the cautions at the
bottom — sources genuinely disagree.

> Last reviewed: 2026-06-20.

---

## Official Kosovo institutions (most authoritative)

- **Komuna e Gjakovës — Historiku** `[SQ]` — official municipal history (founding, the
  1662 trade-representative population figure, 1999 war losses).
  <https://kk.rks-gov.net/gjakove/qyteti-2/historiku-2/>
- **Komuna e Gjakovës — portal** `[SQ]` — current municipal site.
  <https://gjakova.rks-gov.net/>
- **Trashëgimia Kulturore e Kosovës (TKK / DTK)** `[SQ/EN]` — the Department of Cultural
  Heritage's official heritage database; search for listed monuments and protection status.
  <https://dtk.rks-gov.net/>
- **Ministria e Kulturës, Rinisë dhe Sportit — Dept. of Cultural Heritage** `[SQ/EN]`.
  <https://www.mkrs-ks.org/?page=1,10>
- **Këshilli i Kosovës për Trashëgimi Kulturore (Kosovo Council for Cultural Heritage)**
  `[SQ/EN]`. <https://kktk.rks-gov.net/>
- **Regional Centre for Cultural Heritage, Gjakova (Qendra Rajonale për Trashëgimi
  Kulturore)** — the local heritage authority for Gjakova; primary contact for monument
  records and imagery (no stable public URL — approach the institution directly).

## Heritage organisations

- **Cultural Heritage without Borders (CHwB) Kosova** `[EN/SQ]` — NGO that has done
  conservation work in Gjakova's old town (the bazaar, the Hadum Mosque 2003–05, several
  Dukagjin kullas); publishes detailed restoration reports.
  <https://chwbkosova.org/>
- **Monumentet Kulturore — "Tërësia Urbanistike Çarshia e Madhe"** `[SQ]` — MKRS-supported
  heritage-documentation project. Lists the Grand Bazaar as architectural monument
  **E.K. 59/55**, founding ~1594/95 (Selman/Süleyman Hadum Aga's endowment), Evliya Çelebi's
  1662 account, and the gunsmith/craft guilds. Narrative, not technical drawings.
  <https://monumentetkulturore.com/gjakove/teresia-urbanistike-carshia-e-madhe/>
- **Monumentet Kulturore — "Kulla e Sahatit, Gjakovë"** `[SQ]` — heritage page for the clock
  tower (1597 origin on "Fusha e Sahatit"; ~30 m; stone tower with a wooden belfry and lead
  roof). <https://monumentetkulturore.com/gjakove/kulla-e-sahatit/>
- **Archnet (Aga Khan Documentation Center, MIT)** `[EN]` — scholarly records of the Hadum
  Mosque complex and the **Hadım Süleyman Ağa Library** (library founded 1595, its building
  dated 1733; ~200 manuscripts + ~1,300 rare books lost in 1999).
  <https://www.archnet.org/sites/6081> — see also the **Final Report on the 2003–05
  restoration of the Hadum Mosque** (CHwB-Sweden + Packard/KCHP):
  <https://www.archnet.org/publications/9840>

## Primary historical source

- **Evliya Çelebi, _Seyahatnâme_ (1662)** — the earliest detailed eyewitness description of
  Yakova/Gjakova (its bazaar, ~300 shops, baths, tekkes). English translation in **Robert
  Elsie's _Texts and Documents of Albanian History_**: "1662 | Evliya Chelebi: Seyahatname —
  a Journey through Northern Albania and Montenegro." `[EN]`
  <http://www.albanianhistory.net/1662_Chelebi/index.html>
  *(If the http host is unreachable, try `https://` or the Wayback Machine. See the cautions
  on the "nightingale-nest" phrasing and the "Yakova Kalesi" wrong-city trap.)*

## Academic

- **Muzaffer Karaaslan — "Osmanlı Duvar Resimlerine Balkanlar'dan Bir Örnek: Hadım Camii"**
  (Hacettepe Üniv. Edebiyat Fak. Dergisi 36/2, 2019, pp. 352–368) `[TR]` — the authoritative
  study of the Hadum Mosque's **wall paintings** (cypresses, vases, the mosque's own
  self-portrait), the founder **Hadım Süleyman Ağa Bizebân**, the **1594/95** date, and the
  repair history. <https://isamveri.org/pdfdrg/D00221/2019_2/2019_2_KARAASLANM.pdf>
- **Jeta Limani — "Kulla of the Mazrekaj Family, Dranoc"** (Cultural Heritage without
  Borders, 2007) `[EN]` — case study of a Dukagjin tower-house; good on the kulla plan
  (the *oda*, *divanhane*, *frëngji*) and the 500+ kullas lost in 1998–99.
  <https://www.lth.se/fileadmin/hdm/alumni/papers/CMHB_2007/Kosova___Jeta_Limani_-_Kulla_of_Mazrekaj_Family.pdf>
- **"Seyahatnâme Penceresinden Kosova ve Civarı"** `[TR]` — scholarly reading of Evliya
  Çelebi on Kosovo. <https://www.researchgate.net/publication/330006238_Seyahatname_Penceresinden_Kosova_ve_Civari>
- **"Bashkësia islame e Gjakovës gjatë luftës së dytë botërore (1941–1945)"** `[SQ]`.
  <https://www.researchgate.net/publication/378809517_Bashkesia_islame_e_Gjakoves_gjate_luftes_se_dyte_boterore_1941-1945>
  Accessible companion text (with the **1942 census** — ~39 mosques, 14 tekkes of six dervish
  orders — and the 1947 madrasa closure): **IslamGjakova.net, "Nënmyftinia e Gjakovës gjatë
  Luftës së Dytë Botërore."** <https://mail.islamgjakova.net/artikulli.php?id=15308>
- **András Riedlmayer — Kosovo cultural-heritage destruction survey (2000)** `[EN]` — primary
  post-war documentation of the **Hadum Süleyman Aga library**: founded 1595 (building 1733),
  and the **~200 manuscripts + ~1,300 rare books** in Arabic, Ottoman Turkish and Aljamiado
  burned on 24 March 1999 — including the unique Albanian *Mevlud* of Hafiz Ali-Riza Ulqinaku.
  <http://www.bndlg.de/~wplarre/back000307a.htm>
- **Human Rights Watch — "Under Orders: War Crimes in Kosovo" (2001)** `[EN]` — the
  authoritative account of the 1999 war in Gjakova/Djakovica (the burning of the old town;
  ~200 killed in the town, 24 March–12 June 1999).
  <https://www.hrw.org/legacy/reports/2001/kosovo/undword-06.htm>
- **"Attack against Mehmed Ali Pasha" — the Action of Gjakova (Sept 1878)** `[EN]` — the
  League of Prizren's **first military operation**, led by Gjakova's Ahmet Koronica and
  Sulejman Vokshi. <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_against_Mehmed_Ali_Pasha> ·
  <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/League_of_Prizren>

## Turkish / Ottoman-heritage sources

- **Osmanlı Eserleri — Hadım Ağa (Hadumit) Camii, Yakova** `[TR]` — structured record of the
  mosque, its wall paintings, and repair phases (1935, 1947, 1972, 1977–81, 2003–05).
  <https://www.osmanli-eserleri.com/eser/hadim-aga-hadumit-camii-yakova-gjakova-kosova>
- **TYB — "Camiler ve tekkeler şehri Yakova"** `[TR]` — essay framing Gjakova as a city of
  mosques and tekkes (gives 33 mosques, 16 in the centre). <https://www.tyb.org.tr/camiler-ve-tekkeler-sehri-yakova-13135h.htm>
- **Fikriyat — İsmail Güleç, "Bülbül Yuvası: Yakova"** `[TR]` — readable essay on Yakova's
  mosques and tekkes. *Note:* its title-phrase ("nightingale's nest") is a **modern
  paraphrase**, not Evliya's epithet for Gjakova — see the cautions.
  <https://www.fikriyat.com/yazarlar/ismail-gulec/2024/09/21/bulbul-yuvasi-yakova>
- **Diyanet Haber — "Balkanlarda Türk Olmak: Yakova Notları"** `[TR]`.
  <https://www.diyanethaber.com.tr/balkanlarda-turk-olmak-yakova-notlari>
- **TİKA (Turkish Cooperation and Coordination Agency)** `[TR]` — Ottoman-monument
  restoration programmes across the Balkans. *Note:* the recent Yakova restorations (the
  Hadum library, the Sefa / Molla Yusuf / Kusari mosques) were carried out by **VGM**, not
  TİKA — see below. <https://tika.gov.tr/>
- **Vakıflar Genel Müdürlüğü (VGM — Turkey's Directorate of Foundations)** `[TR]` — the body
  behind the recent reconstruction of the **Hadım Süleyman Ağa Library** (~2016) and several
  Yakova mosques (~2018). <https://www.vgm.gov.tr/>
- **TRT Avaz, _Devrialem_ — "Kosova'da Osmanlı Eserleri / Yakova / Hadum Ağa Camii"** `[TR,
  video]`. <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0KexC2npJbY>

## ⚠ Cautions & disambiguation (read before writing content)

- **Wrong-city trap #1 — TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi.** Its "Yakova" entry redirects to
  **"Diyakova," which is Đakovo in CROATIA**, not Kosovo's Gjakova. Do **not** cite İA's
  Yakova/Diyakova for this project.
- **Wrong-city trap #2 — Yakovalı Hasan Paşa Camii** is in **Pécs, Hungary** (founder was of
  Yakova origin). Not a Gjakova monument.
- **Wrong-city trap #3 — Evliya's "Yakova Kalesi."** The fortress Evliya describes at length
  (in the Pojega/Požega sancak) is **Đakovo, Croatia** — confirmed in his own text. The
  genuine Gjakova passage appears as **"Jakovičse / Jakova"** in the Albania leg (Cilt 6).
  Don't mix the two.
- **Evliya's "nightingale-nests."** In the _Seyahatnâme_ this is a **simile for Gjakova's
  shops** ("about 300 shops like nightingale-nests"), **not** a name for the city. The
  standalone epithet *bülbül yuvası* that some modern Turkish writing applies to Yakova is
  Evliya's stock phrase for *other* towns (e.g. Novi Pazar, Dupnitsa). Quote the shops
  simile; don't call Gjakova "the nightingale's nest" and credit it to Evliya.
- **Pristina conflation.** Evliya's "300 shops + Eski Hamam" figures cited in some studies
  describe **Pristina** (his 1660 route), not Gjakova.
- **Hadum Mosque date.** Sources give **1574** vs **1594/95**. Prefer **1594/95** (matches the
  monument documentation and Turkish scholarship); flag 1574 as a minority reading.
- **Mimar Sinan attribution.** The claim that Sinan designed the Hadum Mosque is
  **doubtful/unverified** and chronologically impossible to take literally — **Sinan died in
  1588**, before the 1594/95 build. Treat as legend.
- **Hadum library ≠ Great Madrasa.** The Hadum-complex library/medrese (Süleyman Aga's
  vakëf, 1595) is **separate** from the **Great Madrasa (1748**, founded by Veysel Efendi).
  Both burned in 1999; don't conflate them. The complex's **medrese was destroyed in 2000 by
  a botched restoration** — distinct from the 1999 fire.
- **Recent restorers.** The recent Ottoman-heritage restorations in Yakova (the Hadım
  Süleyman Ağa Library, rebuilt ~2016; several mosques) were carried out by Turkey's
  **Vakıflar Genel Müdürlüğü (VGM)** — **not** TİKA.
- **Clock tower.** Built **1597** (not the "1812–1820" of some travel sites). Its bell/
  clockwork was carried to **Montenegro in 1912**; the tower was **destroyed in 1999**; the
  present tower is a reconstruction of the **2000s–early 2010s** (sources vary: 2005 / 2009 /
  2012). The widely-reported **2001 clock-bell theft is Pristina's** Sahat Kulla, not
  Gjakova's.
- **Tailors' Bridge date.** Genuinely uncertain — likely a **late-15th-c.** crossing
  **rebuilt to its present form in the 18th c.** (the tailors'-guild inscription belongs to
  that phase). Give a range, not a fixed year. Keep it distinct from Gjakova's smaller
  tanners' bridge.
- **Old Hammam.** It **no longer stands**; the date of loss is contested (**WWII vs 2008**),
  and there is **no visitable hammam** in Gjakova today (don't confuse it with restored baths
  in Pristina, Peja, Durrës or Istanbul).
- **Radoniq Lake.** An **artificial reservoir** (built 1977–86, filled 1983), **fed by the
  Lumbardhi i Deçanit — NOT the Erenik**; the **second-largest** body of water in Kosovo
  (after Gazivoda/Ujmani). Its feeder canal is linked to a 1998 ICTY case that is **absent
  from the lake's nature/tourism identity** — omit from cultural-nature content unless
  directly relevant.
- **Accursed Mountains.** **Gjeravica (2,656 m) is Kosovo's highest peak**, but the
  **range's** highest is **Maja Jezercë (2,694 m), in Albania**. The high summits and glacial
  lakes sit in **Deçan/Junik**, not Gjakova municipality — write "reachable from Gjakova,"
  not "Gjakova's."
- **City origin.** "Marketplace mentioned in 1485" vs "town (kasaba) from 1594/95" both
  circulate; present them as two stages, not rivals.
- **1999 bazaar fire — figures.** The documented loss is **431 of ~525 buildings** in the
  Çarshia (cite **431**, not "about 430"). The fire was **24 March 1999**; the destruction was
  *completed in 2000*, when the burned Hadum library/medrese remains were bulldozed in a
  botched restoration (the Saudi Joint Relief Committee) — a **second, distinct event** from the
  war fire.
- **Hadum library rebuild — year.** Academic scholarship (Karaaslan) dates the VGM
  reconstruction to **2016**; the official VGM record says **2018**. The builder is **VGM, not
  TİKA**. Use "2016" (or "the late 2010s") and credit VGM.
- **The Action of Gjakova (1878).** Gjakova's anti-Ottoman siege of Mehmed Ali Pasha was the
  **League of Prizren's first military operation** — a solid, well-sourced fact. But keep its
  cast precise: **Haxhi Zeka** belongs primarily to the later **League of Peja (1899)** and is
  tied to 1878 Gjakova mainly as a **Great Madrasa graduate**; **Bajram Curri** is an
  early-20th-century figure — keep him out of the 1878 frame.
- **Names.** Gjakovë/Gjakova (sq) = Yakova (tr) = Đakovica/Ðakovica (sr). Çarshia e Madhe =
  the Grand/Old Bazaar. Use endonyms per locale.
