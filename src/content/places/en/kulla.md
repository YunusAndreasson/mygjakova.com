The **kulla** is the signature dwelling of the Dukagjin highlands: a square tower of dressed stone, narrow on the ground and opening upward to the *oda* — the guest room where law, story, and hospitality were kept.

Their small, high windows and heavy walls speak of centuries when a house had to be a refuge as much as a home.

## Reading a kulla

Look for the carved doorway, the projecting upper storey, and the *oda*'s long bench — the architecture of a culture that measured itself by how it welcomed a guest.
