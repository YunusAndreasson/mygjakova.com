The **Grand Bazaar** — *Çarshia e Madhe* — is where Gjakova's story has always been told: in the rhythm of hammers, the smell of cut wood, and the slow trade of saddlers, filigree-smiths, and felt-makers along its cobbled spine.

Grown up around the Hadum Mosque from the late sixteenth century, the bazaar became the commercial engine of the Dukagjin plain, its guilds binding the city together.

## What to look for

Walk the full length slowly. Notice the low timber shopfronts, the worn thresholds, and the way the arcade frames the light at either end of the day.
