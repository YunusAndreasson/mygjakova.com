The **Hadum Mosque** — *Xhamia e Hadumit* — anchors the Grand Bazaar. Built at the close of the sixteenth century, it gathered around it the shops, the *hammam*, and the library that made this corner the heart of Gjakova.

Its single dome, slim minaret, and finely painted porch are among the finest examples of late-Ottoman building in Kosovo.

## A place that endured

Damaged and restored more than once across the centuries, the mosque remains a working place of worship and a touchstone for the city's memory.
